Privacy & Data Protection

Delete Account & Data Deletion Policy

Service: Lingowire Effective date: 2025‑11‑23 Last updated: 2025‑11‑23

This Delete Account & Data Deletion Policy explains how Lingowire handles the deletion of your account and associated personal data when you choose to close your account from within the app or by contacting us. This policy should be read together with our general Privacy Policy.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

For the purposes of this policy, “we”, “our” or “us” refers to Lingowire (the “Service”). We act as the data controller for the personal information you provide when using the app.

If you have any questions about this policy or about deleting your account, you can contact us at:

Email: privacy@lingowire.com
Subject line: “Delete Account Request – Lingowire”

2. How you can request account deletion

You can request deletion of your Lingowire account in the following ways:

  • In‑App Delete Account Option: by using the “Delete Account” option in the profile or settings section of the app. We will treat this as a direct request to delete your account and associated personal data, subject to the rules described below.
  • By email: by emailing us at privacy@lingowire.com from the email address associated with your Lingowire account and clearly asking us to delete your account.

For security reasons, we may ask you to verify your identity (for example by confirming access to your registered email) before we complete the deletion.

3. What happens when you delete your account?

When you confirm account deletion, we perform a series of actions designed to remove or irreversibly de‑identify your personal information:

  • Account credentials: your authentication record (such as email and password or third‑party login identifiers) is deleted or de‑linked so you can no longer sign in.
  • Profile information: personal profile fields such as your name, profile photo, bio, languages, and other profile metadata are permanently deleted or anonymized.
  • Usage data linked to your identity: where possible, we delete or anonymize usage data (such as session history or progress metrics) that can reasonably be linked to your account.
  • Social & interaction data: we will remove your direct identifying information (name, avatar, contact details) from conversations, calls, classes, or other collaborative features. However, we may retain anonymized or aggregated records that are necessary for:
    • the integrity of other users’ histories and statistics,
    • security, fraud prevention, and abuse detection, and
    • legitimate business analytics that no longer identify you.

Once your account has been deleted, this process is irreversible. You will not be able to recover your account, conversations, or any associated content.

4. Data that may be retained after deletion

In some cases we may retain limited information even after you delete your account, but only where we have a lawful basis to do so. Examples include:

  • Legal and regulatory obligations: records required to comply with applicable law, tax, accounting, or other regulatory requirements.
  • Security, fraud & abuse prevention: logs and internal records needed to detect, investigate, or prevent security incidents, fraud, or abusive behavior.
  • Aggregated or anonymized data: data that has been aggregated and/or irreversibly anonymized so that it can no longer be used to identify you (for example, statistics about total call minutes across all users). This type of data is not considered personal data under most privacy laws.

Any such retained data is kept only for as long as necessary for these limited purposes and is handled in accordance with our general Privacy Policy.

5. Processing time for delete requests

We aim to process in‑app and email delete requests as quickly as reasonably possible.

  • In most cases, your account and associated personal data will be deleted or anonymized within 30 days of your request.
  • In complex cases (for example, if your account is involved in an active investigation), processing may take longer. Where legally permitted, we will inform you if we cannot fully comply with your request and explain why.

Some data may remain in backups for a limited period of time. Backups are automatically overwritten on a scheduled basis and are not used for active processing except in cases of disaster recovery.

6. Children’s accounts

Lingowire is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law in their jurisdiction. If you believe that a child has created an account in violation of our terms, please contact us at privacy@lingowire.com. We will take appropriate steps to delete the account and associated personal data.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location and applicable privacy law, you may have additional rights, such as:

  • the right to access the personal data we hold about you;
  • the right to correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • the right to restrict or object to certain processing;
  • the right to portability of certain data; and
  • the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, or if you have questions about how account deletion affects your data, please contact us at privacy@lingowire.com.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Delete Account & Data Deletion Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, our services, or applicable laws. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide additional notice within the app.
