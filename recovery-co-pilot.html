Privacy Policy

Recovery Co Pilot

Recovery Co Pilot helps people with sobriety and recovery. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices. By using the app, you agree to these practices.

Information we collect

  • Account info: Email and authentication credentials (email/password or Google sign-in).
  • App content you add: Journal entries, moods, attachments you upload, step progress, meetings, sponsorship notes.
  • Device & app data: Basic device/app info (e.g., OS, version) for performance and security.
  • Usage data: Non-identifying analytics to improve stability and features.

How we use information

  • Provide core features (auth, journaling, step tracking, meetings, sponsorship tools).
  • Generate supportive content with AI prompts (Gemini) without making medical claims.
  • Improve reliability, performance, and safety.
  • Support account management, security, and abuse prevention.

How we share information

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We use trusted processors to run the app (e.g., Firebase for auth/storage/analytics, Google GenAI for text generation).

Data storage & retention

  • Data is stored in Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage). Local caches may be kept on your device.
  • You can delete your account to remove associated data (see in-app “Delete Account”).

Your choices

  • Access/update your information via your account.
  • Delete your account to remove associated cloud data.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and accounts are intended for teens/adults in recovery support contexts.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards (encryption in transit, access controls). No system is 100% secure, so please use a strong password and keep your device secure.

AI-generated content

AI features (Gemini) generate supportive text. We do not use AI for diagnostics or medical decisions. Do not rely on AI content for medical or emergency situations.
