Lingowire Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how Lingowire keeps your data protected so you can focus on learning.

Last updated: November 23, 2025

Introduction

Lingowire helps people connect and practice new languages through voice calls, group sessions, and interactive content. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

What We Collect

We collect profile details you share with us—such as your name, language preferences, country, timezone, and availability. When you join calls or classes, we may store call metadata like start and end times to show your speaking history.

How We Use Your Information

Your information allows us to match you with partners, recommend classes, and personalize your learning experience. We never sell your data. We only use it to power Lingowire features, improve the product, and keep the community safe.

Sharing & Transparency

Lingowire only shares limited details with other learners—such as your display name, profile photo, languages, and availability. We will disclose information to authorities only when required by law or to protect users from harm.

Your Controls

You can update availability, country, timezone, and other profile details anytime. Notification preferences live in your device settings. If you want to delete your account or export your data, contact support@lingowire.com.

Keeping Data Safe

We store information using encrypted services and apply industry-standard security practices. Only authorized team members can access personal data to deliver Lingowire features.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy as Lingowire grows. When changes are significant, we will notify you in-app or by email. Continuing to use Lingowire after updates means you agree with the revised policy.
